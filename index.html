FAQ

Common
Questions

Do I need an appointment?

Appointments are recommended so you can choose a time that suits you. Walk-ins are welcome when a barber is available.

Do you accept walk-ins?

Yes, walk-ins are welcome, subject to availability. Booking ahead is the best way to avoid a wait.

What forms of payment do you accept?

We accept cash and major credit and debit cards. Please check with the shop if you have a question about a payment method.

How long does a haircut take?

Most haircuts take around 30 to 45 minutes. Allow a little extra time for a beard trim or a more detailed service.

Do you offer services for kids?

Yes, we offer haircuts for kids. A parent or guardian should accompany younger clients during their visit.

Are you hiring barbers?

We are always interested in meeting skilled barbers. Contact the shop to introduce yourself and ask about current opportunities.
